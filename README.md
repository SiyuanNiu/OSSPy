# OSSPy
This study develops an operator-splitting based PHREEQC–Python coupling framework (OSPPy), in which electrokinetic processes, solute transport, and equilibrium and kinetically controlled reactions are decomposed into sequential computational substeps and coordinated externally through Python. And OSPPy is presented with three benchmark cases: CK, a cation-exchange model; ISL, a CO₂–O₂ in situ uranium leaching model; and EK-ISL, an electrokinetically enhanced CO₂–O₂ in situ uranium leaching model.The overall workflow of the OSPPy framework is illustrated in Fig. 1.
A key advantage of this framework is its ability to overcome the limitation of PHREEQC that electrokinetic processes and reactive solute transport cannot coexist within a single model, while also enabling flexible integration of geochemical reaction databases
